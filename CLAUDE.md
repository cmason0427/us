# What Us is for (read this before building anything)

Us is a private app for two people, Charlie and Parker (plus their dogs, Kodo
and Wiley). Its whole point, in Charlie's words:

> The whole point of this is to stay in soft contact in a way that relieves
> pressure and makes us not have to ask for things, kind of. Like for food,
> Parker can request a food that sounds good and I can gently decline to
> get/make it, OR he can come home to it, and nobody had to feel bad for asking
> or saying no. Or updates where it's "I want you to see and know about this,
> but not spam your texts, so look at this at your leisure." And just to
> simplify and destress the elements of life that don't need to be stressful.

What that means when designing a feature:

- **Low pressure by default.** Asking and declining should both feel easy and
  blameless: soft wording ("not on the menu", "maybe another time"), quiet
  notifications, nothing that nags or guilt-trips. A "no" should quietly
  disappear, not linger as a rejection.
- **At your leisure, not urgent.** Prefer things you glance at over things
  that demand a reply. Feed posts and pushes are gentle; opt-in where it's
  just nice-to-know.
- **Simplify.** Fewer taps, sensible defaults, nothing required that doesn't
  need to be. If it adds stress or admin, rethink it.
- **Two people, equal footing.** Either person can do anything shared; the
  private things (personal to-dos, private fantasies) stay truly private and
  are enforced in the database (RLS), not just hidden in the UI.

The user (Charlie) is direct and wants blunt, honest feedback and to be asked
when a decision is genuinely theirs to make.
